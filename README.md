# Where welfare entitlements fail, stage by stage

An interactive appendix to Meesho Foundation's research on welfare access in India.

It follows a benefit from the rules to the household: eligibility, application, enrolment, fulfilment and receipt, plus verification across every stage. It names 19 points where entitlements fail and ranks them by size × evidence strength × how movable they are (maximum 45). Click any box to open the next level: what we see, evidence, how it fails, cause, possible intervention, who controls the change, why it ranks there, and the research behind it.

**Open the map:** https://akshatlathi-meesho.github.io/DBT_MF/

Sources are named under every number: HCES 2023-24, NFHS-5 2019-21, PLFS 2025, NSS health survey 2025, CAMS 2022-23, NSS Multiple Indicator Survey 2020-21, Global Findex 2025, official scheme records, CAG audits and published studies. Data as of 10 Oct 2026.
